# Project objectives and deliverables

1. Separate ORIGINAL and AUGMENTED code, settings and results.
2. Provide exactly three execution entry points: RUN_ORIGINAL, RUN_AUGMENT and RUN_ALL.
3. Use English names, documentation, messages, comments and report labels.
4. Preserve the original numerical reconstruction and the complete 7 BUILD / 3 TEST protocol.
5. Retain full source recordings, including startup zeros, without resampling.
6. Export original models, signals, states, six-axis charts and numerical fits without movies.
7. Generate 100 variants per parent: 34 bias, 33 position and 33 combined; 1,000 total.
8. Apply displacement length <= 0.5 m, acceleration bias +/-10 m/s^2/axis and gyro bias +/-0.2 deg/s/axis.
9. Give every variant its own full CSV, SVG/PNG chart, HTML page and six-axis metrics.
10. Make reports readable through high-contrast text, larger labels and vector zooming.
11. Support safe continuation through the same three execution commands.
